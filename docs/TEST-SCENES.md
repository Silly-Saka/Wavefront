# Test scenes

Build these in a flat creative world to check Wavefront by ear. Set **Debug Readout** to Action Bar to see each sound's volume factor ("gain"), treble factor and path length.

| Scene | How to build it | What you should hear |
|---|---|---|
| Piston behind a wall | A stone wall 9 wide and 4 tall, standing alone in the open. A piston on a redstone clock 3 blocks behind it. Stand 4 blocks in front. | Clearly audible, quieter and duller than in the open, and coming from over the top of the wall. Switch Sound Physics off to compare. |
| Friend behind a doorway | A long wall with a 1 wide, 2 tall opening. A sound source (or a friend on voice chat) behind the wall, 5 or 6 blocks along from the opening. | The sound comes from the opening, not from the wall. Walking to the opening makes it louder and brighter. |
| Speaker on a balcony | A platform 4 blocks up. A jukebox on top, 3 blocks back from the edge. Stand underneath. | Music is audible underneath and seems to come from the platform edge. Bass survives better than treble. |
| Fence and bars | A jukebox behind a row of fences, then behind iron bars. | Almost no change compared with nothing in the way. |
| Materials | A jukebox sealed in a 1-thick box. Rebuild the box in stone, planks, glass and wool. | Wool blocks most, then stone, then planks, then glass. Raise **Wall Strength** and every box gets quieter. |
| Sealed room | A sound source sealed in a 2-thick stone box with no gaps. | Effectively silent at the default Wall Strength. |
