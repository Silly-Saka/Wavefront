# Test scenes

Build these in a flat creative world to check Wavefront by ear. Set **Debug Readout** to Action Bar to see each sound's volume factor ("gain"), bass and treble factors and path length.

| Scene | How to build it | What you should hear |
|---|---|---|
| Piston behind a wall | A stone wall 9 wide and 4 tall, standing alone in the open. A piston on a redstone clock 3 blocks behind it. Stand 4 blocks in front. | Clearly audible, quieter and duller than in the open, and coming from over the top of the wall. Switch Sound Physics off to compare. |
| Friend behind a doorway | A long wall with a 1 wide, 2 tall opening. A sound source (or a friend on voice chat) behind the wall, 5 or 6 blocks along from the opening. | The sound comes from the opening, not from the wall. Walking to the opening makes it louder and brighter. |
| Speaker on a balcony | A platform 4 blocks up. A jukebox on top, 3 blocks back from the edge. Stand underneath. | Music is audible underneath and seems to come from the platform edge. Bass survives better than treble. |
| Fence and bars | A jukebox behind a row of fences, then behind iron bars. | Almost no change compared with nothing in the way. |
| Materials | A jukebox sealed in a 1-thick box. Rebuild the box in stone, planks, glass and wool. | Wool blocks most, then stone, then planks, then glass. Raise **Wall Strength** and every box gets quieter. |
| Sealed room | A sound source sealed in a 2-thick stone box with no gaps. | Effectively silent at the default Wall Strength. |
| Music next door | A jukebox in a sealed room with plank walls 1 thick. Stand outside, 3 blocks from the wall. | Mostly thump: the bass is clearly louder than the rest. The debug line shows a number after the plus sign in "bass 1.00 +0.05". Turn **Full Bass** off and the sound goes thin. |
| Pillar | A note block on planks (the bass instrument) on a repeater clock on the ground, heard from 8 blocks away. Then the same note block on top of a pillar 12 blocks up, heard from the ground 8 blocks from the foot of the pillar. | Thinner on the pillar: it has lost the bounce off the ground. The debug line shows "(wave, N ms)" when the wave result was used; N is how long the last run took. Turn **Bass Waves** off and the bass factor goes back to 1.00. |
| Room corners | A closed stone room 5 by 5 and 3 high with the same note block in one corner. Walk slowly from wall to wall. | The bass is a little stronger near the walls and weaker in the middle, a few dB at most. Watch the bass numbers in the debug line change as you move. |
| Bass through a doorway | A wall to the ceiling of a closed room, with a 1 wide, 2 tall doorway. A note block on planks on a clock 6 blocks behind the wall, to one side of the doorway. | Low notes come through noticeably better than with a higher-pitched instrument. Put a door in the doorway and shut it: the bass drops within about a second. |
| Piston upstairs | A stone floor one block thick with a closed room under it. On top of the floor, a piston on a redstone clock, pushing sideways. Stand in the room below. | A dull thud from the ceiling on every stroke, with little of the piston's click. The debug line ends in "(through blocks)". Turn **Sound Through Blocks** off and it is nearly silent. |
| Wool under the piston | The same, with the piston standing on a block of wool laid on the floor. Then with a plank floor in place of stone. | On wool the thud all but disappears. Through planks it is louder than through stone. |
| Seeing the paths | Turn on **Show Sound Paths** (General, Tools) and repeat any scene above. | A red line where the straight path is blocked, yellow lines round the edges the sound bent round, and orange boxes on the blocks a thud travelled through. If nothing is drawn at all, tell the author: the drawing could not be tried before release. |
| Seeing the bass | Turn on **Show Bass Field** with a note block playing nearby. | Dots at chest height round you: white in the open, blue behind walls and in side passages, pink to red against the walls of a room. |
| Sound dots | Turn on **Sound Dots** (the Sound dots row). Stand near a doorway with a mob or a note block on a clock in the next room, out of view. | Marks appear on the floor and walls round the sound and on whatever part of them you can see through the doorway. Red crosses for hostile mobs, green triangles for animals, purple diamonds for note blocks, light blue dots for blocks. Walk away: fewer and fainter marks. Nothing shows through a solid wall except a small patch on the wall itself when the sound comes through it. |
